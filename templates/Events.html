Interschool Basketball Championship, 2023

Educationkhoj, in association with Agilo Foundation & Abhikalpana, successfully organised the Interschool Basketball Championship, bringing together young sporting talent from across Dwarka.

The tournament witnessed enthusiastic participation from 15 leading schools, making it a grand celebration of sportsmanship, teamwork, and competitive spirit. The championship matches were intense, showcasing excellent skills, determination, and passion for the game.

After a thrilling series of contests, ITL Public School, Dwarka emerged as the champion, lifting the First Prize of ₹5,100. The Second Prize of ₹3,100 was secured by GD Goenka School, Vasant Kunj, while the Third Prize was jointly shared by Maxfort School and GD Goenka School, Dwarka, both receiving ₹1,100 each.

The event not only recognised the winning teams but also celebrated the efforts of all participating schools. With the success of this event, Educationkhoj and Agilo Foundation reaffirm their commitment to promoting sports and nurturing young talent for a brighter and healthier future.

Felicitating Government School Students for CBSE Excellence

Educationkhoj, in association with Agilo Foundation, organised a felicitation ceremony to honour the outstanding achievements of government school students who performed exceptionally well in the CBSE examinations.

The initiative was aimed at celebrating their hard work, dedication, and perseverance, while also motivating other students to strive for academic excellence. Meritorious students were awarded certificates and prizes as a token of appreciation for their remarkable performance. Teachers and parents were also applauded for their constant support and encouragement in shaping the success of these young achievers.

The event created a sense of pride and inspiration, showcasing that with determination and the right guidance, students from government schools can achieve great heights. Educationkhoj and Agilo Foundation remain committed to recognising talent and providing platforms that encourage learning, confidence, and success among students.

Career Counselling Session for Government School Students

Educationkhoj, in collaboration with Agilo Foundation, organised a Career Counselling Session exclusively for government school students with the aim of guiding them towards informed career choices.

The session was designed to create awareness about diverse academic and professional opportunities available after school, helping students align their interests and strengths with the right career path. Experienced counsellors and experts interacted with the students, addressing their queries related to higher education, competitive exams, skill development, and emerging career fields. Special emphasis was laid on building confidence, setting realistic goals, and exploring opportunities in both traditional and modern sectors.

The initiative proved to be highly impactful as students gained valuable insights into career planning, scholarships, and vocational options. The session concluded with words of motivation, inspiring young learners to dream big and work with determination. Through this effort, Educationkhoj and Agilo Foundation reaffirmed their commitment to empowering students from government schools with the right guidance for a brighter future.

Olympiad by Educationkhoj & Agilo Foundation, 2013

Educationkhoj, in association with Agilo Foundation, successfully organised an Olympiad designed to inspire academic excellence and healthy competition among government school students.

The Olympiad aimed at testing conceptual understanding, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills across various subjects. Students from different schools actively participated, showcasing their knowledge and determination to excel.

The Olympiad not only served as a platform to identify bright young minds but also encouraged them to push their boundaries and prepare for future challenges. Top-performing students were recognised and rewarded with prizes and certificates, while all participants were appreciated for their hard work and enthusiasm.

The event reflected Educationkhoj and Agilo Foundation's commitment to nurturing young talent and promoting a culture of learning beyond textbooks. The Olympiad concluded with an atmosphere of encouragement, motivating students to continue their pursuit of knowledge with confidence and passion.

Talent Hunt – Encouraging Young Minds, 2010

Educationkhoj, in association with Agilo Foundation, successfully organised a Talent Hunt for students of Classes X, XI, and XII. The event aimed at recognising and rewarding young talent by providing them a platform to showcase their academic brilliance through a specially designed examination. A large number of students enthusiastically participated, making the competition vibrant and intellectually engaging.

The contest tested their knowledge, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities. The highlight of the Talent Hunt was the felicitation ceremony where meritorious students were rewarded with attractive cash prizes. The First Prize of ₹5,100 was awarded to the top performer, while the Second Prize of ₹3,100 and Third Prize of ₹1,100 went to the next best achievers. To further encourage participation, consolation prizes of ₹500 each were awarded to 11 students who showcased remarkable effort and potential.

The event was not only about prizes but also about igniting confidence and inspiring students to aim higher. Educationkhoj and Agilo Foundation remain committed to creating more such opportunities that nurture academic excellence and foster a spirit of healthy competition among students. The Talent Hunt concluded with cheers, motivation, and the promise of a brighter tomorrow for the budding generation.
