About Education Khoj

For over 12 years, Educationkhoj has been at the forefront of educational engagement and youth empowerment. Beginning with our widely recognized educational tabloid, we have consistently delivered high-quality, informative, and inspiring content aimed at students and parents. Our publication has served as a trusted resource for thousands of students, helping bridge the gap between academics and real-world learning. Over the years, we have evolved into a multifaceted organization that not only informs but also inspires and activates learning communities.

What We Do

Educational Tabloid – for the past 12 years our flagship tabloid has been a trusted name in the academic community, dedicated to connecting schools, institutions, students, and parents through meaningful and impactful content. With a strong presence in the education sector, we serve as a vital platform that not only shares knowledge but also offers promotional opportunities to educational institutions and brands.

Our monthly tabloid features:

  • Latest Educational News & Updates
  • Stay informed with current developments in the education sector, including board exam results, curriculum changes, and new government policies.
  • Achievements & Highlights
  • We showcase outstanding performances of students, schools, and teachers, giving them the recognition they truly deserve.
  • Events & Competitions
  • Coverage of academic and sports events, quizzes, workshops, and seminars organized across institutions.
  • Educational Advertisements
  • A prime space for schools, colleges, coaching classes, publishers, and edtech companies to reach a targeted and engaged audience.
  • Career & Exam Guidance
  • Expert advice, career tips, and information on competitive exams to help students plan their futures wisely.
  • Parenting & Student Corners
  • Engaging content for parents and students to promote better understanding, learning, and development. Over the years, our tabloid has grown into a respected publication not only for its content but also for its role in promoting educational growth. Today, we proudly partner with schools, educational institutions, and learning-focused businesses to provide them with the visibility they need to flourish.
